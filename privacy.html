Ranks Uploader – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Ranks Uploader is a private desktop script used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Ranks & Rations to upload and schedule the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses these scopes:

  • youtube.upload: upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • youtube.force-ssl: set thumbnails, upload captions and add videos to the owner's playlists.

The app reads only the basic channel information needed to confirm which channel is signed in.

How data is used and stored

  • Video files, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and captions come from the owner's own computer and are sent only to YouTube.
  • The OAuth token is stored locally on the owner's computer. It is never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • The app has no server, collects no analytics, uses no cookies and does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Ranks Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services and is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes the app's access on that computer.
